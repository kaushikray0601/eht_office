Calculation basis

Formula reference and calculation evidence trail.

These guide cards summarize the active SR calculation basis; the detailed assumptions and limitations remain in the manual below.

Mean Insulation Temperature

Tmean = (Tmaint + Tamb,min) / 2

Default conductivity basis for the current calculation release.

Conductivity Polynomial

k = A · T2 + B · T + C

Evaluates insulation conductivity using stored material coefficients.

Base Heat Loss

qbase = 2πk(Tmaint - Tamb,min) / ln((2t + D) / D)

Calculated before heat-loss safety factor and final design heat loss.

Spiral Factor

SFspiral = Qdesign / Plow-voltage

Used to check whether a candidate tracer can satisfy heat delivery limits.

Heat-transfer physics

What the current heat-loss engine represents.

The present release is intentionally transparent about implemented physics and future model depth.

01

Conduction Through Insulation

The implemented base model evaluates heat flow through cylindrical insulation using pipe outside diameter, insulation thickness, maintain temperature, minimum ambient temperature, and insulation conductivity.

02

Conductivity Basis

The active default evaluates conductivity at mean insulation temperature. Placeholder methods remain visible for future standard/vendor tables and integrated k(T) calculation.

03

External Effects

Wind correction is included as a practical project factor. Full convection, radiation, emissivity, jacket material, and solar/environmental effects are future model extensions.

04

Review Evidence

The result package distinguishes base heat loss, heat-loss safety factor, and design heat loss so reviewers can see what was calculated and what was added as design margin.

Standards alignment

IEEE and IEC controls are treated as engineering checkpoints.

The software supports traceability and review; it does not replace project-specific code compliance, listed equipment, or responsible engineer approval.

IEEE 515 / IEEE 515.1

Industrial and commercial trace-heating design logic is supported through explicit electrical and thermal design basis fields, catalogue qualification checks, circuit sizing evidence, and maintenance-friendly deliverables.

IEC/IEEE 62395

Industrial and commercial system-design guidance is reflected by traceable inputs, heating-device selection records, termination-aware cable quantities, and output packages suitable for review and maintenance.

IEC/IEEE 60079-30-1

For explosive atmospheres, catalogue suitability checks include hazardous-area classification, temperature class, gas-group compatibility, exposure limits, and declared manufacturer limits where data exists.

IEC 60079-14

Installation design and selection controls are supported through area classification data, equipment selection evidence, documentation trail, and review notes for hazardous-area projects.

SLD and power topology

Electrical output is treated as a topology workflow, not a static drawing.

The guide prepares users for calculation-backed SLD review, topology edits, cable schedules, and future model-aware navigation.

Generated SLD Baseline

Each calculated line produces topology data for circuits, protective devices, junction boxes, branches, heat-tracing feeders, and downstream connections.

Combine and Split

Users can review combine-feeder and split-circuit operations with recalculated breaker recommendations and topology audit records before applying changes.

Cold Cable Engineering

Cold cable and feeder quantities are driven by the active topology, circuit count, junction-box structure, and cable schedule assumptions, rather than a disconnected estimate.

Model Integration Path

The IDF viewer becomes the bridge to 3D review: line focus, component identity, routing context, and future spatial checks can connect back to calculation evidence.
